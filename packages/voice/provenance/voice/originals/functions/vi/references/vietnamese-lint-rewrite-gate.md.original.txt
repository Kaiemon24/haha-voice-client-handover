# Vietnamese lint–rewrite–re-lint gate

|---|---|---|

Owner: `viet-natural-decision-editor/v0.2`. This reference adapts the useful
mechanism from SlopMonster without importing its English vocabulary or score.
For the detection catalogue, examples and limits, read [Nhận diện văn tiếng
Việt có dấu hiệu máy móc](vietnamese-ai-sounding-detection.md).

## Order

1. Freeze the final merged reader text and its source claims.
2. Run `scripts/check_vietnamese_release.py`.
3. Classify each finding as a lexical/translation, structure/rhythm,
   source-bound proof, or final-merge signal.
4. Repair only the flagged wording, using the meaning-first workflow and
   preserving numbers, conditions, actors, certainty, names, links, and
   machine values.
5. Run the checker again after the last merge and keep the receipt beside the
   report evidence.

## Statuses

- **BLOCKED:** meaning drift, dropped condition, changed number or actor,
  unsupported proof, or a sentence that cannot be reconciled to its source.
- **WARNING:** translation-shaped order, abstract slogan, mechanical rhythm,
  unnecessary generic English, or lowercase sentence opening.
- **PASS:** no blocker remains and all changed units were checked against
  source truth. PASS is a release status, not a numerical naturalness score.

The checker is deterministic evidence. It does not replace a Vietnamese reader
review, a read-aloud pass, or the report-format source/render gates. Terms owned
by a report template, such as `Input standard` and `Output standard`, may be
passed in the explicit allowlist and must not be translated by this gate.

The catalogue's patterns and counts are review signals only. They do not decide
whether a sentence was written by AI, and they must not rewrite a legal quote,
proper name, technical key, or intentional brand line.

## Source-bound proof

Quantities and social proof require a source-claim list. A number beside
`khách hàng`, `người dùng`, `đánh giá`, `tin tưởng`, `hài lòng`, or a similar
proof noun is BLOCKED when the exact claim is absent from that list. This is a
source check, not a fluency judgment.
