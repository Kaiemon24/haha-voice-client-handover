## 1. Purpose and scope

Voice owns selection and application
of voice for reader-facing English and Vietnamese across web, apps, sheets,
documents, reports, slides and design copy. Apply even without a design system
or an explicit voice request. Content and format owners retain their work.
This is Voice’s shared output module, not a brand personality.

## 2. Inputs

Use the task language, reader, situation, immutable facts and any selected
approved design system or scoped voice choice. Read the actual applicable
voice rules and their version/hash, not only a system name, visual preset,
binding ID or newest file. No extra brand interview is required for baseline
or apply-only work. Ask only when a conflict changes correctness or authority.

## 3. Workflow

1. Preserve higher instructions, facts, permissions, exact user scope and
   approved wording. Explicit task-specific voice overrides remain scoped;
   do not mutate their source profile.
2. If this output applies a selected brand profile (including one supplied with a design system), use that system's approved
   applicable voice as the controlling voice. Read its actual rule text and
   record the system/voice identity, version/hash and task locale in evidence.
   Do not infer personality from colors, typography or competitor copy.
3. If no applicable approved voice exists, read `brand/voice-baselines.md` and apply
   its EN or VI rules as a neutral language baseline. For a system with missing
   voice or partial locale coverage, retain its valid rules, fill only missing
   language mechanics from the baseline and record the gap. Never claim this
   fallback is learned brand voice or a complete system guideline.
4. EN and VI are default supported languages, not a requirement to duplicate
   every deliverable. Apply the requested language; for mixed outputs select
   each surface's language. Without a stated language use task/reader context;
   do not silently switch to English. Extra locales retain their extension
   and evidence limits under `brand/voice-baselines.md`.
5. Before drafting, pass the selected rules to the content/format owner.
   Apply to headings, body, labels, cell notes, chart text, buttons, help,
   empty/error states and accessibility copy. Preserve machine-only keys,
   formulas, code, paths, quoted source wording and locked official labels.
6. After final data merge, load `functions/vi/README.md` for Vietnamese
   quality and meaning preservation. Its profile calibration is optional
   for explicit profile work, not a competing selector or a fresh intake gate.
   For English reader copy load `functions/en/README.md`; retain
   any applicable domain review. Reviewers preserve the selected approved voice
   while checking clarity, truth and language. No average style score can
   waive meaning drift or a material voice violation.
7. Read `output-handoff.md` and bind the final merged copy receipt.
   Recheck selected voice, situation, terminology and source facts after edits.
   For visual output inspect current native/rendered copy and important states;
   instruction or lint success alone is not release proof. Keep selection and
   review evidence internal unless requested or a material gap affects delivery.

## 4. Tools and permissions

Scoped reads and local drafts only. Applying voice grants no profile promotion,
system creation, sample upload, installation, runtime mirror, publication,
external send or instruction mutation. Missing/stale selected rules block the
claim that the selected voice was applied; continue only a clearly labeled
neutral draft when compatible with the task. Do not overwrite a locked voice.

## 5. Output

Deliver the requested artifact in its existing format. Task evidence records
voice source (selected system, scoped explicit choice or neutral baseline),
language, actual rule references/version/hash, gaps, meaning review and
native/rendered proof when relevant. Do not add process narration to product
copy or change report structure to expose this receipt.

## 6. Verification and stopping

Check baseline EN/VI with no system; selected system with distinct approved
voice; missing/partial voice; explicit one-language and mixed-language tasks;
stale binding; exact labels/formulas; report structure and existing approval
boundaries. Compare actual output to frozen facts and applicable voice traits.
Rule-presence checks prove routing text only; same-agent examples do not prove
universal future compliance, learned authenticity or native-language quality.
Stop readiness claims at the exact missing evidence boundary.

## 7. Supporting material

- `brand/voice-baselines.md`: mandatory language mechanics for baseline and gaps.
- Selected system guideline: mandatory actual approved rules when bound.
- `brand/voice-and-tone.md` and `brand/voice-evidence-method.md`: only for discover,
  create, learn, adapt, profile review/update or evidence conflicts; not a
  mandatory full guideline build for ordinary copy application.
- `functions/vi/README.md`: internal Vietnamese quality and meaning gate.
- `functions/en/README.md`: internal English review.

Required missing dependencies block their branch; never fabricate or silently
replace them. Portable handovers include actual selected rules and necessary
language review instructions, not hidden OS calls or project paths.
