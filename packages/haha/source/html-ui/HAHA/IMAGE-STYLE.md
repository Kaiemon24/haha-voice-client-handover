## Style locks

- Neutral monochrome: black, white and gray; no sepia, blue cast or colored accent.
- Real editorial photography of purposeful work, with calm gestures and credible anatomy. People and spaces are illustrative unless verified source photos are provided.
- Soft daylight, moderate contrast and preserved shadow detail; avoid silhouettes and theatrical light.
- Matte cloth, plaster, wood and unbranded practical objects; select a few relevant objects rather than filling the image.
- Eye-level medium-wide framing for spaces; overhead framing for working surfaces only when the task needs it. Keep faces, hands and key objects clear.
- Reserve a quiet text field before generation. In this master, place short separate text within x5–34%, y12–62%; never cover the people, hands or laptop. Check text contrast at the actual crop.

## Apply to slides and websites

- Slide background: use this wide master whole at 16:9. Place editable black type in the left field; use a separate text panel if contrast is not enough. Never bake titles or logos into the AI image.
- Website hero: keep a wide crop that preserves the two heads, hands and work object. Keep navigation and action outside the photo when possible; text stays in the quiet field.
- Mobile: show the entire wide photo and place copy in a separate block. Do not squeeze the subject into a tall cover crop. Generate a separately reviewed portrait scene if a portrait composition is essential.
- New subjects: reuse style and material/light grammar; do not repeat these people or their exact shot for unrelated business stories. Use verified real product/office/team media whenever factual identity is required.
- Do not upscale without approval for the exact accepted original. Derivatives record master hash, crop and dimensions; a changed master invalidates them.

## Typography in the previews

- Slide source canvas: 1280 × 720; title 64 px, body 24 px, label 20 px. The displayed miniature scales these roles uniformly by rendered width / 1280.
- Website source composition: 1280 px wide; title 48 px, body 20 px, label 16 px. Its miniature scales with the same factor; actual website implementation uses the declared reading roles at destination size.
- Narrow layout below 700 px: separate copy uses title 32 px and body/label 16 px. Navigation keeps 16 px. These are actual reader sizes, not scaled miniature text.
- Title, body and utility label use the declared regular400 role; inline emphasized keywords use700. No added600/800 roles.
- Do not copy the miniature pixel sizes into the final slide or website. Read the role values in DESIGN.md and check actual destination size.

## Acceptance checks

Check subject relevance, neutral grayscale, plausible anatomy, readable texture, quiet field, protected faces/hands, crop at intended destination, text contrast and source lineage. Provider success alone does not pass. This section is local-trial guidance; rights and shared-default admission remain separate.
